# Changelog

## 1.0.0 - Initial release

- Hand pump, intake strainer hose, outlet nozzle hose, 1 m / 2 m hoses and a hose elbow (Hammer > KY-Items).
- Pump tar out of tar pits: single strokes or hold E to keep pumping, with full pumping animation (hands on the lever, the body bends with the stroke) seen by every player.
- Only the liquid is removed; items in the pit stay. Drained pits seep back over time.
- The tar travels visibly through see-through hoses and pours out of the nozzle with a stream, splashes, a puddle and sounds.
- Pieces can be tilted and rolled while building.
- Hose look per hose (solid / see-through) plus a world-wide override.
- `[General] EnableAll` switches the mod off while playing.
- English and German.
- Works with the add-on WaterAndTarPools (pools for the pumped tar, water pumping, water styles, pool signs).
